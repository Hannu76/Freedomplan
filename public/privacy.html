FreedomPlan ("FreedomPlan", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use FreedomPlan, we may collect information such as:

Account Information

If you create an account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number, where required
  • Login and authentication information
  • Account preferences

Financial Planning Information

When you use financial planning tools, you may voluntarily enter information such as:

  • Income or expected income
  • Expenses
  • Rent
  • Travel costs
  • Education-loan amounts
  • Repayment assumptions
  • Savings targets
  • Budget information
  • Currency or financial planning inputs

This information is used to provide the planning tools and calculations you request. FreedomPlan does not provide personalised financial advice merely because you enter financial information into the platform.

Payment Information

If you purchase a paid FreedomPlan plan, payment processing may be provided by a third-party payment provider such as Razorpay.

FreedomPlan does not need to store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN, or banking credentials in order to process your payment. Payment information is handled through the payment provider's payment infrastructure and is subject to the provider's applicable terms and privacy practices.

We may receive transaction-related information such as:

  • Payment identifier
  • Payment status
  • Amount
  • Currency
  • Order identifier
  • Purchase date
  • Plan purchased
  • Information required to associate a successful purchase with your account

Technical Information

When you access FreedomPlan, technical information may be processed, such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Operating system
  • Pages accessed
  • Basic usage information
  • Error and diagnostic information

The exact information collected depends on the technologies and services used by the live website.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide FreedomPlan features
  • Save your planning information
  • Process purchases
  • Verify payments
  • Provide premium access
  • Generate reports requested by you
  • Maintain security
  • Prevent fraud and misuse
  • Improve the platform
  • Respond to support requests
  • Communicate important service information
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We will not use your personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or, where required, your consent.

3. Payment Processing

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers. For example, if Razorpay is used, the payment transaction may be processed through Razorpay's systems.

FreedomPlan does not ask users to provide their card PIN, UPI PIN, or banking password to FreedomPlan. Never send your banking password, card PIN, UPI PIN, or authentication credentials to FreedomPlan through email, chat, or any other communication channel.
